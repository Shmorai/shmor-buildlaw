Builder gone bust? What contractor insolvency means for you

Construction has more insolvencies than most industries. When a party in the chain fails, the contract terms, the timing and a few statutory rules decide who can do what next.

By the BuildLaw editorial team · 28 September 2026 · 1 min read

Photo: Ümit Yıldırım on Unsplash

If your contractor becomes insolvent (clients)

  • Standard forms usually let the employer terminate on the contractor’s insolvency, and often suspend payment obligations until the final account. Section 233B of the Insolvency Act 1986 doesn’t stop a client terminating an insolvent contractor.
  • Secure the site, materials and design documents; check bonds, guarantees and warranties.
  • Get a new contractor to finish, and keep records of the extra cost, which may be set off against sums otherwise due.

If your client or main contractor becomes insolvent (suppliers)

  • Section 233B, added in 2020, generally stops a supplier (including a contractor or subcontractor) from terminating, or doing anything else, because its customer has entered an insolvency procedure. You may have to keep working, though the officeholder must pay for supplies after the insolvency.
  • “Pay when paid” clauses are generally banned, but the Construction Act allows them where a party further up the chain is insolvent.
  • Unprotected retention is usually lost into the insolvency, one reason for the proposed retention ban.
  • You’ll normally prove in the insolvency as an unsecured creditor for sums due before it.

Adjudication and insolvency

A company in liquidation can refer a dispute to adjudication (Bresco v Lonsdale [2020]), but enforcement may be refused where cross-claims and insolvency set-off make it unfair. Take specialist advice early.

Frequently asked questions

Can I stop work if the main contractor goes into administration?
Often not because of the insolvency itself, because of section 233B. You may still have rights if payments due after the insolvency aren’t made. Take advice before stopping work.
