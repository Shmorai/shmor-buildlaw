The Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) explained

CIS is HMRC’s tax scheme for the construction industry. Contractors deduct tax from subcontractors’ payments and pass it to HMRC. It sits alongside, not inside, the Construction Act payment rules.

By the BuildLaw editorial team · 30 September 2026 · 2 min read

On this page

Who it applies to

CIS covers most construction work on buildings and civil engineering, including site preparation, demolition, alterations, repairs and installing heating, lighting, power, water and ventilation. It doesn’t cover, for example, architecture and surveying, scaffolding hire without labour, carpet fitting or delivering materials. Contractors (including some businesses that spend heavily on construction) must register; subcontractors should register.

Deduction rates

Subcontractor statusDeduction
Registered20%
Not registered30%
Gross payment status0%

Deductions apply to the labour element, not to materials, VAT or CITB levy. Contractors must verify new subcontractors with HMRC.

Monthly returns

Contractors file a monthly return by the 19th after the end of each tax month (which runs from the 6th to the 5th). In months with no payments, file a nil return or tell HMRC you’ve temporarily stopped using subcontractors. Late returns attract penalties, starting at £100.

Gross payment status

Subcontractors who meet HMRC’s business, turnover and compliance tests can be paid gross. As of April 2026, HMRC has stronger powers to remove gross payment status where a business knew or should have known that a payment was connected with fraud, with a longer wait before it can reapply. Check the current tests and rules on GOV.UK.

Reverse charge VAT

Since 1 March 2021, the VAT domestic reverse charge applies to most supplies of construction services between VAT-registered businesses that are reported under CIS: the customer accounts for the VAT instead of the supplier. It doesn’t apply to supplies to end users. Remember CIS and VAT are separate from payment and pay less notices, so show deductions clearly.

Frequently asked questions

Is CIS the same as a pay less notice deduction?
No. CIS is a tax deduction passed to HMRC. A pay less notice is a contractual and statutory mechanism to pay less than the notified sum. Keep them separate on payment documents.
